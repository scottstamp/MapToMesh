    Welcome to MapToMesh
    
    
    Turn real LiDAR elevation data into 3D-printable, multi-colour terrain and city models.
    
    
      
      
    • Pick any area on Earth: a coastline, a mountain, your neighbourhood. Canada, the US and France
        get classified LiDAR point clouds; much of Europe, Japan, New Zealand and parts of Australia get
        0.5-5 m elevation models; everywhere else uses the 30 m Copernicus model.
      • 
      
    • Elevation comes from public LiDAR surveys (NRCan CanElevation and USGS 3DEP); water, roads and
        building outlines come from OpenStreetMap.
      • 
      
    • You get a 3MF file with one part per colour, ready for Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer,
        with flat water, clean shorelines and an optional frame, label and separate base plate.
      • 
    
    
    
    Get started: search a place or drag the box on the map, pick the size and
      colours, then press Generate 3MF.
    
    
    Free for personal use, and you can sell prints of the models you make (credit the map data, see the
      license).
      Source code and issues: github.com/scottstamp/MapToMesh
    

    
    
      
    Help keep it running
    
      
    MapToMesh is free, but hosting it isn't: every model is built on the server, and the elevation and
        map data it downloads take bandwidth and storage. If it saved you time, or you're printing something
        you love, a donation helps keep it online and free for everyone. Thank you!
